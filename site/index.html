What the report cannot establish

Owner funding, initial capital, deposits, and transfers: these are funding movements, not earned revenue. Operator receipt classifications are not independently reconciled to exclude them.

Realized returns: not established by paper settlements. Unrealized value: not realized profit. Internal allocation: a resource policy, not an audited balance.

Provider costs: reservations and estimates are not settled invoices. Full economic profit needs complete, attributed costs and reconciled cash.

05 / EVIDENCE BEFORE AUTHORITY

Maturity is earned.
Authority is granted.

Paper evidence, better calibration, or a successful deployment cannot unlock financial execution on their own.

01 Observe and establish baselines

Collect timestamped public observations. Preserve missing data. Record market consensus and a PASS baseline before claiming independent skill.

02 Test on later outcomes

Count distinct resolved events, include costs, and evaluate calibration out of sample. Repeatedly scoring the same evidence does not create new evidence.

03 Allocate bounded research

Only new evidence can support promotion. Failed calls retain reservations. A weak specialist can lose attention or be quarantined.

04 Separate authorization gate Locked here

Live-value execution needs explicit authorization, a supported venue adapter, deterministic risk limits, secret isolation, reconciliation, and recovery. This public site provides none of that authority.

06 / TRENCH-1

Survival before selection.

A research track for early token survival. Its job is to test whether information available at the decision time distinguishes later outcomes.

  1. t₀ / OBSERVATION

    Freeze what was knowable

    One earliest observation per token. Preserve the information boundary.

  2. t₁ / CONTROL

    Match the experiment

    Compare contemporaneous candidates and controls, not a convenient retrospective sample.

  3. t₂ / OUTCOME

    Wait for the label

    Chronological outcomes, cohort integrity, and walk-forward evidence precede any claim.
