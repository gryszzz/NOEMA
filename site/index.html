TEVBot Trading Terminal

TEV Bot Trading Terminal is a self-hosted, multi-agent trading terminal running on the NOEMA agent runtime. Crawlers sweep Kalshi, Polymarket US and on-chain markets; specialist agents forecast; every forecast is scored; and nothing touches real money unless a fail-closed gateway, a capital guard and the owner all say yes.

Honest status: TEV runs in paper mode. No agent beats the market baseline yet, and no profit has been demonstrated.

01 / WHAT IT DOES

Rooms of crawlers. Desks of agents. One judge.

TEV v6 trading room

A live neon room: agent wall, crawler bay with a filters → holders → judge → entry funnel, decision stream, prediction-market board, news wire, treasury, autonomy & safety panel and per-source system health.

News crawler v2

29 configured feeds, primary sources first: White House, Federal Register, Congress.gov, GovInfo, Fed, SEC, DOJ, CDC, NOAA Hurricane Center, ECB, Bank of England, CourtListener, plus BBC, NPR, Guardian, Al Jazeera, CNBC, Politico, The Hill and GDELT. Targeted searches for the soonest-closing markets, 5-minute cadence, robots.txt respected, crawled text treated as untrusted.

Matching precision: 15/16 hand-labelled links (≈94%), up from 25%. A small sample.

Accuracy engine

Every forecast goes into a ledger before its outcome. Resolved forecasts are scored per agent: Brier score, log loss, calibration and EV. CHIEF weights each agent by sample size × measured skill, agents still out of bounds after 30 resolved forecasts are benched, and TEV JUDGE skips when its own samples disagree.

Capital guard + equity feed

75% rising floor, 10% daily stop, 3 / 5 / 8% edge-scaled sizing with quarter Kelly. A read-only equity feed totals Kalshi, Polymarket US, Solana and EVM balances every 5 minutes. Any failed source marks the reading stale and blocks trading, and deposits raise the floor instead of counting as profit.

Desks & venues

  • Kalshi markets + account, gated live orders
  • Polymarket US markets + gated live adapter (off)
  • Solana trench crawler, paper desks; Jupiter signer merged, ships disabled
  • EVM KyberSwap signer for ETH/Base/Polygon, merged, ships disabled

TEV Mac app

A native macOS app with the TEV logo opens the private, password-protected dashboard from your Dock. Self-hosted: keys live only in a separate signer process, never in logs or model context.

X / social signal feed coming

In progress. The SOCIAL seat is a research-only placeholder today and does not influence trades.

02 / HOW THE SAFETY WORKS

Fail-closed by default. Autonomy is earned.

Fail-closed gatewayEvery live flag ships at 0. Missing config, stale data or a failed check refuses the order.
75% rising floorTrading halts if equity falls below 75% of its peak. The floor only ratchets up, and deposits raise it too.
10% daily stopA 10% drop from the day's opening equity halts trading until the owner resets it.
Owner reviewLive trading requires explicit owner-review flags that only the owner sets, plus address pins and a token allowlist (empty refuses all).
Fresh equityNo equity snapshot in the last 15 minutes means no live order.
Edge-scaled sizingQuarter-Kelly within tier caps: 3% per trade, 5% after ≥100 resolved forecasts beating the market, 8% after ≥200.
