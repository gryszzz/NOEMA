**Verdict: no strategy passes. One veto (e, `orcACR`) passes as a veto, but the strategy it filters still

passing veto anyway (it is still negative at every size).**

## Data (frozen)
- pump.fun on-chain tape from epochs **1062–1064** only: 2026-10-19 05:30:15 ET to 2026-10-22 11:06:37 ET
  (77.6 h). It has 7,333,952 curve trades, 1,294,515 blocks and 408,044 wallets. The backfill job is still
  adding older epochs (1061, 1060, 1059). Those epochs are **excluded** because the study is frozen to the
  epochs named in the pre-registration.
- Universe U: SOL-quoted, standard (non-mayhem) curve coins **created inside the window**. That is 73,641
  coins, of which 1,915 completed or migrated. Excluded: 53,139 non-SOL-quoted launches and 33,963 SOL
  mayhem launches.
- Split: early = epochs 1062–1063. Held-out = epoch 1064 (slot ≥ 459,648,000, from 2026-10-21 09:36 ET).
  Measured slot time is 0.2156 s.
- Fills follow the pre-registration. Entry happens at signal slot + L (L=5 primary; L=2, L=10 and $5 size are
  sensitivity runs). The fill price uses the curve reserves after the last trade at or before the target
  slot. Costs are a 1.25 % fee per side, a 1 % buffer per side, and 0.0005 SOL per transaction. Our own
  position shifts the reserves on the sell. A coin that completes is sold into the final curve. Size is
  0.1 SOL. I hand-checked one B1 trade, and the engine matches the manual calculation to 1e-10.

## Multiple testing
- **178 pre-registered early-split tests** form one family (a 36, b 60, c1 32, c2 15, d 6, e 8, f 16, g 3,
  h 2). There were 241 evaluations in total, counting held-out, controls and sensitivity runs.
- Holm < 0.05: **4** tests, all in idea e (`B1|orcACR` p=4.4e-20, `name_seen`, `creator_prior`,
  `dev_buy_gt4` Holm 0.018).
- Bonferroni (p×178) < 0.05: the same 4.
- BH < 0.05: **6** (the 4 above, plus h2 shield and d `B1|veto_all_dead_ge1`, each at BH 0.014).
- Held-out results use Holm across the selected strategy ideas, and separately across the selected veto ideas.

## META shield re-score on on-chain prices (h)
- **h1** (166 closed META paper positions with on-chain coverage). On-chain open→close return net of costs has
  a median of **−8.7 %**, ex-top-3 mean −16.9 %, and a hit rate of 2.4 %. The raw mean (+3,094×) is meaningless.
  Position 32 carries a balance-delta price artifact of +537,497×, and NOEMA itself marked it "no fresh
  price (unpriced, excluded from P&L)". Dropping the 10 positions NOEMA marked unpriced (a labelled
  sensitivity, not the pre-registered test) gives n 156, mean **−14.3 %** [−18.2, −10.6]. NOEMA's own
  recorded realized mean was −4.2 % (n 156, median −2 %), so **NOEMA's marks understate losses by about
  10 pp**. Feed verdicts: 85 had no on-chain trades during the hold, **36 were FROZEN_FEED** (NOEMA's price
  was flat while the chain moved), 28 NOEMA prices moved, and 17 were roughly flat on both.
- **h2** (19,732 META matches; 3,630 in U and in the window; 3,015 executable on the curve; time1800 exit).
  Shield-pass n 1,146, mean −5.9 % [−7.1, −4.5]. Shield-fail n 1,869, mean −8.8 %. Welch p 0.00048 (Holm
  0.083, BH 0.014). The shield ranks coins in the right direction, but coins that pass it still lose. For
  rank=first matches, all 581 passed the shield, so that comparison is empty.

## Paper desk and scaling
- **No paper desk wired.** The pre-registration only allows a desk when every criterion is met. For a veto,
  that includes the vetoed strategy being profitable on held-out data. The `orcACR` veto raises B1 from
  −10.8 % to −8.6 %, which is still a loss.
- Scaling: none is owed, because nothing is desk-eligible. For completeness, I ran it on the passing veto
  (kept B1, held-out n 2,860). Mean net was −8.6 % at 0.1 SOL, −7.6 % at 1 SOL, −7.4 % at 5 SOL and −7.2 % at
  10 SOL. That is 130 kept signals per hour, with up to 19 positions open at once. It looks less negative at
  larger size only because the fixed 0.0005 SOL transaction fee matters less. The own-impact model shifts
  reserves only for our own position and ignores how other traders react, so large-size numbers are
  optimistic. It is negative at every size.
- Actionable takeaway, as research only: `orcACR` (skip creators who launched before in the window, repeated
  name+symbol, or a creator initial buy > 4 SOL) is a robust **filter** worth keeping in any future trench
  hypothesis. It cuts about 70 % of B1 signals and improves net by about 2 pp in every epoch and day. It is
  not an edge by itself.

## Deviations and clarifications (all mechanical, none chosen after seeing results)
1. Data frozen to epochs 1062–1064 while the backfill was still adding 1061 and older epochs. The launch
   index already flagged some coins from epoch 1061 as `created_in_window`, so U is also restricted to
   `create_slot` 458,784,000–460,079,999. This applies the pre-registered rule that coins created before
   the window are excluded.
2. B2 features use slot-derived clocks (t0 + Δslot × 0.2156 s) and evaluate `trench_fresh` at 15 s and 45 s,
   as pre-registered. The slot clock is an implementation detail the pre-registration left unspecified.
3. c2: late-period stats were computed for all 15 variants in the same pass and appear in the JSON. Selection
   used only the early split, mechanically. Only the selected variant feeds the verdict.
4. h1: the pre-registered t-test on the mean (p 0.16) is distorted by one price artifact. Median, ex-top-3
   and the "excluding unpriced" sensitivity are reported and labelled. The verdict is FAIL either way.
5. The PumpSwap fee is assumed equal to the curve fee (1.25 %/side), as pre-registered.
6. (a) per-unit test uses the three pre-registered segments (late 1062, 1063, 1064) plus UTC days.
   Sensitivities rerun the copied signals at L=2, L=10 and $5.

## Caveats
- About 3.2 days of data in one market regime. The held-out set is one epoch (about 25.5 h).
- One third of window launches are non-SOL-quoted, and mayhem coins are excluded, so nothing here covers them.
- Fills are simulated from the tape at slot granularity. Real latency, priority-fee auctions and Jito
  bundles would make every result **worse**, not better.
